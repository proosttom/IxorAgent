Title: Ixor Job Posting - LLM and Agentic AI Engineer

Your role

As an LLM and Agentic AI Engineer you work in Ixor's AI team. At Ixor we build
software that makes a difference, from the Flemish Traffic Centre to smart
energy managers. We are heavily involved in custom AI applications and are
looking for a developer with a few years of experience who wants to grow along
in the agentic AI era.

You work in a small, experienced team on concrete AI products: from prompt
engineering and RAG pipelines to autonomous agents that carry out tasks
independently. You not only build smart models, you also build the robust
Java, NodeJs or Python backend and cloud infrastructure around them.

Responsibilities
- Develop AI systems from concept to production
- Integrate LLM models (OpenAI, Anthropic, Azure AI, ...)
- Translate customer problems into the right technology choices: RAG,
  multi-agent architectures, fine-tuning, ...
- Design and manage cloud infrastructure on AWS or Azure
- Write robust, testable code and take part in code reviews
- Contribute to architecture decisions together with senior developers
- Learn quickly and share knowledge via internal tech talks and our annual
  workation

Your profile
- Master's degree in computer science, applied informatics, or equivalent
  experience
- A passion for technology, combined with a strong drive to keep learning
- Experience with Python or TypeScript
- Curious about new developments in AI, with practical experience with LLMs or
  agentic AI
- Basic knowledge of cloud platforms (AWS or Azure); certifications are a
  plus, not a requirement
- Knowledge of Object-Oriented Programming (OOP)
- Experience with frameworks such as LangGraph, AutoGen, ...
- Enjoys working in a small team where you quickly get responsibility
- Fluent communication in Dutch; English is the working language for
  technical documentation

Our approach
- At Ixor we work Agile, using the Lean Startup method with short, effective
  development phases
- Continuous learning
- Teamwork
